# Prop ground snapping

In Inredning, **Snap to ground vid placering** is enabled by default. Turn it off for free wall placement. This is a session tool preference; existing placements are not moved automatically. Select an existing prop and click **Snap to ground** to apply the operation with undo support. It works on native GTA references and imported GLB/YDR decorations. Doors, windows and frames retain their current placement behavior.

Snapping finds the nearest structural floor/surface below the click height (new placements) or prop bottom (selected-prop button). It samples the center and four rotated footprint corners, then puts the model's lower bound at the highest matching support point. A 15 cm upward tolerance handles small placement/rounding errors. It handles upper floors, basements, built room floors and gentle ramps without forcing everything to height zero. Props placed from a wall are positioned on its viewer-facing side when snapping is enabled. No floor found means the existing/free height is kept; the button reports that no floor was found.

Surfaces come from the current imported building geometry and generated interior batches, in shell coordinates. Geometry is cached until those mesh collections change. Near-vertical surfaces and degenerate triangles are excluded; triangle winding does not matter. Cutaway does not change ground detection. Placed props are excluded, preventing self-hits. This operation applies when placing a new prop or pressing the button, not after every gizmo movement; manual height adjustments and wall-mounted decoration remain available.

This is structural visual-surface placement, not a physics collision solver. Flat ledges or the top of a ceiling can be a support surface when below the reference point. On a slope the prop remains upright, and five samples do not simulate individual furniture feet or arbitrary complex footprints. It does not snap objects onto other placed props. Original model dimensions, origins, collision, frozen state and export references remain unchanged; only the placement position/height is adjusted.

Tests cover ground zero, upper floors and roofs above, basements, generated floors, slope/rotated footprint clearance, vertical surfaces, no-hit preservation, original native bounds, snap toggle, unchanged doors and visible UI controls. Existing catalogue/editor regressions pass. No actual FiveM collision test was performed for this editor-only placement change.
